About M Fragrance

M Fragrance is a small, independently owned perfume business based in Olivenhoutbosch, started by Portia Malatji with a simple goal: to make premium-quality scents accessible to everyday customers without the markup charged by big-name fragrance houses.

The product range is built around a few signature notes. Coco Vanilla, a warm blend of coconut and vanilla suited to evening wear, has become the brand's best-selling scent. Saad, a woodier fragrance aimed at men, sits alongside lighter everyday options.
