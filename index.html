About M Fragrance

M Fragrance is a small, independently owned perfume business based in Olivenhoutbosch, started by Portia Malatji with a simple goal: to make premium-quality scents accessible to everyday customers without the markup charged by big-name fragrance houses. What began as a hobby of experimenting with oil blends in a home kitchen has grown into a small production line that now supplies body sprays, hand lotion, perfumes, and scented lotions to customers across the local area and beyond. Every bottle is mixed, tested, and packaged by hand, which means the team can adjust concentration and scent strength based on direct customer feedback rather than mass-market averages.

The product range is built around a few signature notes rather than dozens of forgettable options. Coco Vanilla, a warm blend of coconut and vanilla suited to evening wear, has become the brand's best-selling scent since launch. Saad, a woodier and more amber-forward fragrance aimed at men, sits alongside lighter everyday options like the Body Spray and Hand Lotion lines for customers who want a subtle, all-day scent rather than something designed for a night out. New customers are encouraged to start with the sample-size bottles before committing to a full 80ml purchase, since fragrance preference is such a personal thing.

Special offer: new customers get 15% off their first order this month when they register through the Registration page and mention the promotion at checkout. Beyond the products themselves, M Fragrance is built on personal service: every order is confirmed directly with the customer, delivery timelines are communicated honestly rather than guessed at, and the team is always happy to recommend a scent based on what a customer already wears and likes. Whether the goal is a daily signature scent or a gift for someone else, the Products page below has full details, and the Contact page has everything needed to reach the team directly.
